Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my baby arrives early or later than my estimated due date? +
Babies rarely arrive on an exact schedule. We arrange our bookings with built-in flexibility windows around your due date so your confinement lady is ready to step in when you return home from delivery.
What medical screening is completed before placement? +
Every confinement lady undergoes a comprehensive pre-departure health screening through recognized clinical centers, including physical health checks, chest X-rays, and general health screenings.
How are traditional herbs and grocery ingredients handled? +
We provide you with a recommended grocery and herbal shopping guide prior to arrival. Confinement ladies also assist with meal planning and adapt recipes using fresh, high-quality local ingredients available in your area.
